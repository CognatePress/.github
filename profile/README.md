# Cognate Press

**An imprint, not a platform.**

Six independent technical publications — one language each, one specialist each,
each on its own domain. Cognate Press puts its name on writing by the person who
did the work, and on nothing else.

A press mark is shorthand for a standard. This one asserts exactly one thing, and
it is the narrowest claim we could make: *the person who wrote this does the work
they wrote about.*

**[cognate.press](https://cognate.press/)**

## The standard

Five rules, and every title is held to all of them.

**Mechanism before advice.**
Advice that arrives before the mechanism is a rule you have to trust. Advice that
arrives after it is a conclusion you can check, disagree with, and apply to a case
the article never mentioned.

**Every number carries its method.**
A performance claim arrives with the version, the machine, the command and the
output it produced — or it says plainly that the thing was not measured. "Faster"
without a figure is an opinion, and a benchmark without its method is an opinion
with digits.

**One byline, and it is a real practitioner.**
Every publication carries exactly one name, and that name works in the language it
writes about. A title opens when such a person is ready to sign it, and not before.

**Nothing published here is paid for.**
No sponsored posts, no affiliate links, no vendor writing about its own product
under a byline. When a tool is recommended, the reason is in the article and the
reason is the only reason.

**Corrections are published in place.**
A claim that turns out to be wrong is corrected on the page that made it, carrying
the date and the evidence that changed it. Nothing is quietly rewritten, because a
record that can be edited without a trace is not a record.

## What it is not

- **Not a platform** — there is no way to sign up and publish here.
- **Not an aggregator** — the imprint hosts nothing and reprints nothing.
- **Not an agency** — nothing ghostwritten, nothing syndicated, nothing generated.
- **Not an ad network** — no sponsored posts, no affiliate links, no paid placement.

## How the network is built

**Separate domains, separate builds.** Every title is its own site and its own
deployment. The imprint runs no shared platform underneath them, so a title going
quiet takes nothing else with it.

**A mark with no brand colour.** Historical linguists mark a reconstructed root
with an asterisk. The emblem is that asterisk, drawn in three strokes — always set
in whatever colour the page around it is already using, so it can sit in a footer
the imprint does not control.

**Components, not a template.** What the titles share is a handful of
framework-free blocks — a network bar, a footer grid, a signature — that inherit
the host page's type and colour rather than imposing any. No stylesheet is handed
down. See **[cognate.press/components](https://cognate.press/components)** and
**[cognate.press/identity](https://cognate.press/identity)**.

## About this organization

This organization holds the source behind the imprint and its titles. The source is
not the publication: everything a title publishes lives on that title's own domain,
and that is where it should be read, linked and cited.

**Found something wrong in an article?** Write to the domain that published it —
each title answers at `contact@` its own domain. Corrections are made on the page
that carried the claim, with the date and the evidence that changed it.

**Anything about the imprint as a whole** — rights, press, the newsletter —
goes to **contact@cognate.press**.
