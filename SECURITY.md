# Security Policy

## Scope

The sites the imprint publishes — cognate.press and the domain of each
publication — and the source behind them.

Out of scope: reports generated by an automated scanner and sent without a working
reproduction, missing hardening headers with no demonstrated impact, and anything
that requires a reader to already be compromised.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Write to **contact@cognate.press**. Do not open a public issue for a security
report.

Include what you would need yourself to reproduce it: the affected domain or file,
the steps in order, what you expected, what happened, and any output that shows it.
If a proof of concept is short enough to paste, paste it.

You will get an acknowledgement within five working days, and a decision on the
report — fixed, mitigated, or declined with the reason — once it has been
reproduced.

## Disclosure

Please give us a reasonable window to fix a confirmed issue before publishing it.
We do not run a bug bounty and cannot offer payment. We will credit you by the name
you ask for, or not at all, as you prefer.
